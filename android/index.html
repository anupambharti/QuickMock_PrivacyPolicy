QUICKMOCK · ANDROID

Privacy Policy

Effective November 6, 2026
Provider: Technoforbes · admin@technoforbes.com

QuickMock for Android edits and renders images on your device. This release has no accounts, advertisements, analytics, tracking, or screenshot upload service. Device frames are generated locally and work offline.

Images you choose

You can import images with Android’s system photo or document picker, or send supported images to QuickMock using Android sharing. The system gives QuickMock access to the items you choose. QuickMock does not request permission to browse your entire photo library and does not request camera, microphone, location, contacts, or advertising identifier access.

Smart Screen Fill, screenshot color analysis, framing, backgrounds, redaction, stitching, and export rendering run on the device. They do not send your images or edit information to Technoforbes, a model provider, or a frame service. The app has no active online frame catalog and no app network connection is needed for editing.

Local projects and preferences

QuickMock keeps editable projects, original images, edit settings, and previews in its private Android app storage so you can reopen your work. An editable project can retain its unredacted original even after you redact an export. Delete the project when you no longer want that original stored.

Preferences, such as onboarding completion and editor defaults, are also stored locally. Private app data is excluded from Android automatic cloud backup by the app’s configuration. Android and your device manufacturer control device security and platform behavior.

Redaction and exports

Blur and pixelation are flattened into the exported image pixels. QuickMock re-encodes exports without copying source EXIF or GPS metadata. The unredacted image is not embedded in the finished export as an editable layer. Transparent canvases export as PNG with real alpha; the checkerboard visible in the editor is a guide and is not exported.

You decide whether to save an export to your device’s Photos or Gallery, save it through Android’s file chooser, or share it to another app. Gallery saving uses Android MediaStore to add the finished image and does not require broad photo-library access. File and share destinations receive the image only after your explicit action; their own privacy terms apply. Export copies saved outside QuickMock remain under your control and are not removed when you delete a QuickMock project.

Retention and deletion

Delete one saved project from Recent Mocks, or use Delete All to remove all saved projects and previews. QuickMock retains at most 100 recent projects and removes older entries as that limit is exceeded. Deleting projects does not reset small preferences or remove exported images saved to external destinations.

Temporary import files are removed after processing. Temporary export files are held in app cache and may be removed by cleanup or by Android. Uninstalling the app removes its private local app data according to Android behavior. Technoforbes has no server copy of your images or projects to delete.

Purchases, children, and third parties

All editing and export tools in QuickMock 1.0 for Android are available without an in-app purchase or subscription. The optional QuickMock mark can be removed at no charge. The app contains no advertising, analytics, payment, or third-party data-collection SDK.

QuickMock is a general-purpose creator utility. It does not collect screenshots, projects, or other user content from children through an app service.

Support and public web pages

If you email admin@technoforbes.com, Technoforbes receives your address, message, ordinary email headers, and any attachments you choose to send. The support team uses the information you send to respond to your request. Please avoid including unnecessary private information or attachments.

Opening the public privacy or support page uses your browser. These pages add no Technoforbes analytics, advertisements, forms, external fonts, or third-party scripts. GitHub Pages may process ordinary web-request information to serve the pages under GitHub’s privacy statement. Your browser and email provider may process information under their own policies.

Changes and contact

We will update this policy and its effective date for material changes to QuickMock’s data practices. Contact admin@technoforbes.com with privacy questions.
