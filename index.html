01 · Scope

Who this policy covers

This Privacy Policy describes how Technoforbes handles information when you use the QuickMock iPhone app, its Share Extension, and its Recent Mock widget. In this policy, “QuickMock,” “we,” “us,” and “our” refer to the QuickMock service provided by Technoforbes.

QuickMock is a screenshot creation utility. It does not require a QuickMock account, and the current release does not operate a cloud-sync or image-processing service.

02 · Data collection

What we collect

The QuickMock app does not transmit your screenshots, edits, redaction regions, exports, contacts, precise location, advertising identifier, or Photo Library contents to Technoforbes.

The app contains no advertising, cross-app tracking, analytics SDK, or third-party data-collection SDK. We do not sell personal information and do not use your content to train an artificial-intelligence model.

QuickMock records limited operational values in local preferences, such as whether onboarding is complete and small counters used to understand frame-resolution outcomes on that device. These values are not transmitted to Technoforbes or an analytics provider.

No account or payment information in this release

All current-release tools are available without creating an account or completing an in-app purchase. If a later version introduces purchases or additional online services, we will review this policy and the App Store privacy disclosures before that version is released. Apple would process App Store payment information under Apple’s own terms; QuickMock would not receive full payment-card details.

03 · User content

Images, edits, and local history

Images you choose through Apple’s system Photos picker, Files, or the QuickMock Share Extension are processed locally. Automatic screen fitting, screenshot-matched colors, Smart Screen Fill, redaction, and stitching are computed on your device.

To let you reopen work, QuickMock may store the following in a protected shared app container:

  • the original image you selected;
  • your frame, canvas, layout, transform, shadow, and redaction settings;
  • a flattened preview used by Recent Mocks and the widget.

The main app, Share Extension, and widget use Apple’s App Group mechanism to access only the QuickMock data required for those features. The widget displays a flattened preview rather than an editable project.

QuickMock does not operate an account-based or cloud-sync feature. Apple may include eligible app data in an iPhone or iCloud backup according to your device, backup settings, and Apple’s services.

04 · Device access

Permissions

Choosing images

QuickMock uses Apple’s system picker. The picker gives QuickMock access only to the images you select; it does not give the app permission to browse your entire Photo Library.

Saving to Photos

If you tap Save to Photos, QuickMock requests add-only Photos access solely to insert the finished image. Add-only access does not allow QuickMock to read or browse the library.

Permissions not requested

QuickMock does not request tracking, location, camera, microphone, contacts, or local-network permission.

05 · Network activity

Offline frames and external destinations

In the current release covered by this policy, QuickMock’s optional online frame registry is disabled. Device frames are constructed from bundled procedural geometry and work offline. Your screenshot is not uploaded to find or generate a frame.

If a future release enables an owner-curated frame registry, we will update this policy before release to identify the service, its hosts, purpose, and log-retention practices. Even in that design, screenshot pixels are not intended to be included in a public frame-catalog request.

When you open a frame’s source or license link, use the system share sheet, or choose an external destination, you are directing iOS to another service or app. The destination’s privacy policy applies after the content leaves QuickMock.

This policy website

This static policy page uses no Technoforbes analytics, advertising, web forms, external fonts, or third-party scripts. It is hosted by GitHub Pages, so GitHub may process ordinary web-request information—such as an IP address, browser information, and request logs—to deliver the page under the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

06 · Support communications

Information you choose to email

If you contact admin@technoforbes.com, we receive the email address, message, standard email headers, and attachments you voluntarily provide. We use that information to reply, troubleshoot, maintain security, and manage the support request. We do not sell it or use it for advertising.

Support correspondence is kept only as long as reasonably needed for those purposes and any applicable legal obligations. Please crop or redact sensitive information and avoid attaching a private screenshot unless it is necessary and you are comfortable sharing it.

07 · Output

Redaction, metadata, and export

Blur and pixelation are flattened into the pixels of the exported image. QuickMock re-encodes exports without the source image’s EXIF or GPS metadata. The unredacted source is not embedded as a recoverable layer in the exported file.

If you choose a transparent background, QuickMock exports a PNG with clear or partially transparent pixels. The checkerboard shown in the editor is a visual guide and is not included in the output.

Review before sharing. Your editable project can retain its original image even when the export is redacted. Delete the project or all Recent Mocks if you no longer want the original stored by QuickMock.

08 · Retention

Keeping and deleting data

Local project history remains until you delete the project or remove QuickMock. You can delete an individual Recent Mock or use Delete All from the Recent Mocks menu.

QuickMock keeps at most 100 Recent Mock projects and automatically removes older history entries as that limit is exceeded. Delete All removes project files and previews; small local preferences—such as editor defaults and onboarding state—remain until the app’s container is removed. Temporary exports may be removed by QuickMock or iOS during normal cleanup.

Because QuickMock does not receive a server copy of your screenshots, projects, or exports, we do not have a remote image copy to delete. To remove editable project content, use Delete All. Removing QuickMock and its extensions removes their containers according to iOS behavior.

09 · Safeguards

Security

QuickMock uses iOS app sandboxing, protected app-group storage, bounded image imports, validated frame assets, and locally flattened exports to reduce accidental access or unsafe processing. No method of storage is completely risk-free, so keep your device protected and review sensitive exports carefully.

10 · Children

Children’s privacy

QuickMock is a general-purpose creator utility and is not directed to children for the purpose of collecting personal information. We do not knowingly collect user content or personal information from children through QuickMock.

11 · Updates

Changes and contact

We may update this policy when QuickMock’s features or legal obligations change. Material changes will be reflected on this page by revising the effective date.

For a privacy question or an applicable privacy-rights request, email admin@technoforbes.com. Please do not attach a private screenshot unless it is necessary to diagnose your request and you are comfortable sharing it.
